The Wall at O Street SE

The Design-Build Services for the O Street SE Retaining Wall Restoration project involve the structural rehabilitation and stabilization of the existing retaining wall to ensure long-term integrity and compliance with DGS standards. The scope includes demolition of deteriorated sections, foundation reinforcement, and the construction of a durable retaining wall system designed to withstand environmental and load-bearing pressures. Drainage improvements will be implemented to mitigate water damage and erosion. Additionally, the project includes site restoration, such as grading, landscaping, and sidewalk repairs, to enhance safety and aesthetics.

Coordination with utility providers will be required to address potential conflicts, and all work must adhere to local regulatory requirements and permitting processes. The design and construction approach will focus on cost efficiency, sustainability, and minimal disruption to the surrounding area. SPD Contracting, Inc. will oversee all aspects of project execution, working closely with the design team and stakeholders to ensure timely completion and quality results.
